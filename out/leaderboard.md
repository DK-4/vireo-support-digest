### Why this ranking should not be used to judge people

- **It ranks queues, not agents.** Mean tickets closed per week by team: Logistics 5.3, Returns Desk 5.26, Billing 5.12, Email Frontline 3.52, Chat Frontline 3.07, Voice Frontline 2.78, Escalations & Warranty 1.99.
- **Channel sets the ceiling.** Median handle time in minutes: social 26, voice 27, chat 28, email 28. An email agent cannot out-close a chat agent.
- **It buries Tier 2 by design.** Tier 1 mean 3.81/week against Tier 2 mean 1.99/week. Tier 2 resolves 94.5% of all warranty tickets, and policy section 6 says explicitly that Tier 2 is measured on resolution days and is not to be compared with Tier 1 on volume.
- **Auto-closed tickets count as attendance** (policy section 8), so they are shown in their own column rather than folded into the total.
- Resolver's roster team matches the team the ticket was routed to on 87.8% of tickets.
