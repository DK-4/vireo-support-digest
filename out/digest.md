## What I would do next
1. **Put the 178 over-compensated orders in front of Finance weekly.** Policy section 5 already says a refund and a replacement must never both be given for one order, and that breaches go to Finance the same day. 103 orders got both and 124 were refunded more than once; almost all span several tickets weeks apart, which is why nobody has caught them. Cutting this from 3.52% to 1.0% of ticketed orders is worth about Rs 44,511-Rs 74,716 a quarter.
2. **Fix first response on email.** It accounts for 440 of 1051 breaches (11.6% of its own volume against a 480-minute target).
3. **Look at Delivery & Shipping repeat contacts.** 341 of 2030 resolved tickets in that category came back within 30 days (16.8%), the highest of any category.
